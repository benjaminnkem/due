# Demo script (90 seconds)

Say this, on the live page, with the hash visible.

"A tailor is owed 50 dollars. On Stellar, a payment still goes through if you send 45, or the wrong coin, or you pay late. The invoice is in a chat. The chain only saw tokens move.

Due makes the invoice the thing you pay.

This due is 10 testnet USDC, to this recipient, open until tomorrow. I pay it."

[Pay. Wait. Show the hash. Open Stellar Expert.]

"That transaction moved USDC from the payer to the recipient. The contract did not keep it.

Now I try to pay 9."

[Pay 9. Show WrongAmount.]

"The contract refused. Nothing moved. The hash is the proof.

Due does not fix a wrong address. It fixes the amount, the asset, and the time. Testnet only."
